Version 1.0 is the first public release of MealOrbit.

The launch sequence introduces the product and writes Initial mass targets. Skip with defaults is available.

Command Deck shows Today's burn and the four windows Apogee, Zenith, Perigee and Drift.

Catalog Sweep and Telemetry Scan resolve products from Open Food Facts or the local shelf. Payload Dossier computes This portion from grams. Lock Window assigns eaten today or a future day.

Burn Log, Fourteen-Day Horizon with drag and drop, Acquisition List and Mass Targets are included. An optional Local airlock passcode can be stored on the device. All user data stays local.
