The only permission is the camera. It is requested on Telemetry Scan when Enable camera telemetry is tapped, which presents the system camera prompt. The usage-description string in the build settings is MealOrbit reads barcodes to lock a product into an orbit window. If access is denied or restricted, Telemetry Scan shows Camera is denied or Camera is restricted with an Open Settings button. A Manual barcode or URL field is always present, so the camera is not required. No other permission usage-description keys are set.

There is no login, no in-app purchase, no user-generated content shared with other people, no account deletion flow, and no App Tracking Transparency prompt. Mass Targets includes Reset all data, which wipes the on-device vault after confirmation; that is a local reset, not an account-deletion flow. An optional Local airlock passcode can be stored from Mass Targets with Store passcode. It is not an account. A fresh install has no passcode, so Airlock does not appear unless a previous install left a value in the Keychain.

MealOrbit is a personal calorie and macro food log for people who plan meals across a fourteen-day horizon. Nutrition figures come from Open Food Facts or a bundled local shelf. The launch sequence and Mass Targets state that the app is not medical advice.

No credentials or demo account are needed. On a cold launch the launch sequence appears full screen: Two weeks on rails, Sweep and scan, Mass targets, then Initial mass targets. Tap Skip with defaults, or enter Energy kcal, Protein g, Carbs g and Fat g and tap Write targets. The split shell then opens on Command Deck. The sidebar lists Command Deck, Catalog Sweep, Telemetry Scan, Burn Log, Fourteen-Day Horizon, Acquisition List and Mass Targets. From Command Deck tap Sweep catalog, type a name such as eggs, or on idle Catalog Sweep tap Try Apogee Eggs, select a row to open Payload Dossier, enter Portion mass, tap Lock into a window, choose a Window, set Eaten today or a Future day, and tap Confirm lock. Scan payload opens Telemetry Scan; tap Enable camera telemetry to allow the camera, or type barcode 0072700005005 and tap Lock. Fourteen-Day Horizon is in the sidebar and on the Command Deck teaser; hold a planned payload and drop it on another day or window. Open burn log opens Burn Log. Park on acquisition list from Payload Dossier adds to Acquisition List. Mass Targets edits Daily mass targets and hosts Re-run launch sequence, Reset all data, Local airlock and Contact MealOrbit.

The app contacts Open Food Facts at https://world.openfoodfacts.org/cgi/search.pl for Catalog Sweep and https://world.openfoodfacts.org/api/v2/product/<code>.json when resolving a barcode. Product thumbnails may load from image URLs returned by that database. Contact MealOrbit opens https://mealorbit.pro/contact-us in Safari. Logs, plans, targets, the Acquisition List, cached products and the optional passcode stay on the device. A six-product local shelf is bundled: Apogee Eggs, Zenith Beef Mince 5%, Perigee Smoked Mackerel, Drift Oat Milk, Station Butter and Payload Mixed Nuts.

Behaviour does not differ by App Store region. Catalog calls always use world.openfoodfacts.org. Day keys and number formatting follow the device calendar and locale.
